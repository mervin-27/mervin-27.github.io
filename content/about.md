Mervin Goklas Hamonangan is a Junior Research Associate at LPEM FEB UI, working on macroeconomics, development, and political economy. He is a sectoral author for LPEM FEB UI's Indonesia Economic Outlook and contributes the monthly RDG (Rapat Dewan Gubernur) inflation commentary for Bank Indonesia's Board of Governors meeting.

He has served as a consultant and advisor to public sector institutions, including the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Industry, and Bank Indonesia, as well as organizations such as the World Bank and the Indonesia Business Council (IBC). He holds an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics and Political Science.

Prior to joining LPEM FEB UI, he gained research experience through research assistant positions at institutions including J-PAL Southeast Asia and Lembaga Demografi, and worked as a Project Consultant at Boston Consulting Group (BCG).
