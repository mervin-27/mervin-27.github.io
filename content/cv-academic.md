## Research

**Colonial Education Reforms and Political Ideology post-Independence: The Ethical Policy of Dutch East Indies**
Work in progress (master's thesis) · LSE MSc Extended Essay · Distinction · May 2024
Studies whether Dutch colonial school expansion under the Ethical Policy shaped post-independence support for Islamic- and Communist-based parties in Indonesia's 1955 election, combining the 1930 *Volkstelling* colonial census with district-level 1955 election returns and 1920 pesantren/mosque and Darul Islam rebellion data.
